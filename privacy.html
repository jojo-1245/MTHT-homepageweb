MetaHint Privacy Policy

Metahint Global INC ("Company") values users' personal information and complies with relevant laws including the Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and what measures we take to protect it.

1. Personal Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect the minimum necessary personal information for service provision and obtain separate explicit consent for sensitive information (health and skin data).

  • Account registration and management: email address, password, nickname, mobile number.
  • Health and skin analysis service (required): photos and videos captured via smartphone camera, analysis results (quantified health/skin status).
  • AI consultation service: AI conversation content and search history.
  • Virtual asset wallet service: MTHT coin wallet address, transaction history (Transaction ID).
  • Automatically collected: device information (model, OS), IP address, service usage logs, cookies.

2. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

  • Service provision: AI-based health/skin analysis and results using the camera.
  • AI customized consultation: customized health information and conversational consultation based on analysis data.
  • Financial services: MTHT coin wallet creation, asset management, and transfer/transaction support.
  • User management: identity verification, customer inquiry response, fraud prevention.

3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

We destroy personal information without delay after the purpose of collection and use is achieved. Exceptions:

  • Upon withdrawal: destroyed immediately (except where retention is required by law, e.g. virtual asset transaction records).
  • Retention under applicable laws: records on contracts or withdrawal of offers — 5 years; records on consumer complaints or dispute handling — 3 years; service visit records — 3 months.

4. Processing of Sensitive Information and Biometric Data (Separate Consent Required)

The MetaHint app processes users' facial images, photos, and the health/skin measurements derived from them (sensitive information) for health and skin analysis. We obtain separate explicit consent for each feature, independently from general personal information.

  • Health (biometric) measurement: The facial video captured during measurement is processed in real time on your device using FzljtiliSignal (Binah) technology. The original video is never stored in any form or transmitted externally, and is discarded immediately after processing. Only the numerical measurement results are stored on the company's server.
  • Skin analysis: The facial photo you take is sent to and processed by a third party (YouCam by PerfectCorp) on overseas servers for analysis. The company does not store the original facial photo on its server; it stores only the analysis result values.
  • Purpose of facial data processing: Facial video and photos are processed solely to perform the health/skin analysis requested by the user, and are not used for any other purpose (such as marketing or AI model training).
  • Retention period and reason: Because original facial video and photos are not stored, there is no retention period for them (they are discarded immediately after processing). The measurement result values derived from analysis are retained, for the purpose of allowing users to view and manage their own measurement records, until the user withdraws their account or directly deletes the record in the app, at which point they are immediately and completely deleted. The company does not retain facial data indefinitely.
  • Third-party (YouCam by PerfectCorp) practices: Facial photos transmitted for skin analysis are used by Perfect Corp.'s server solely for analysis processing and are permanently deleted from Perfect Corp.'s server once processing is complete. Perfect Corp. does not store, sell, or trade facial or biometric information, and purges the relevant data upon a user's deletion request. (Per Perfect Corp.'s Privacy Policy.)
  • Retention and deletion: Users can delete individual measurement records directly in the app (deleted immediately and permanently). All related data is destroyed upon account withdrawal.

5. Provision to Third Parties and Outsourcing

The company does not provide personal information to third parties without the user's consent. Before any data is transmitted, users are informed of what is sent, to whom, and why, and consent is obtained per feature. For service provision, we process and outsource as follows.

  • YouCam (PerfectCorp): Processing facial photos for skin analysis (overseas transfer). Equivalent protection measures are applied.
  • MetaHint AI server: Processing conversation content and health/skin measurement summaries for AI chat.
  • Cloud infrastructure (AWS, Google Cloud, etc.): Data storage.
  • FzljtiliSignal (Binah) SDK: On-device processing of facial video (no external transmission).

Users may withdraw consent to the above third-party provision and processing at any time in the app's 'Data Consent Settings'.

6. Procedures and Methods for Destruction

We safely delete information after the purpose is achieved; electronic files are irreversibly deleted and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

7. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives

Users may access, correct their information, or withdraw consent through account withdrawal at any time.

8. Security Measures for Personal Information

We take measures such as encryption (passwords, wallet private keys), SSL for transmission, and access restrictions and training for personnel.

9. Virtual Asset Wallet

MTHT wallet addresses and transaction records may not be fully deletable due to the nature of the blockchain. The user is responsible for managing their private key; we are not liable for loss due to user negligence.
